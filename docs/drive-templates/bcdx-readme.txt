Bacolod x X — discovering and creating new possibilities, ideas,
and businesses from Bacolod.

Purpose
This workspace helps us move from personal goals to validated business candidates without
rushing to choose a single idea. The goal is to build businesses only when they support the
life, values, and impact we actually want.




Workflow

1. Self Analysis
Complete this independently before discussing business ideas with the group.
Use "_TEMPLATE - Self Analysis".
Attach the template and "_initialize prompt" to ChatGPT, then answer one question at a time.
For every question, discuss the answer first. Do not finalize the first response immediately.
The goal is to understand what kind of life you want, what "enough" means, what you will not
sacrifice, and what role work or business should play.



2. Business Ideas & Validation
Each founder may propose multiple ideas. Do not choose a winner too early.
Create one copy of "TEMPLATE - Business Idea & Validation" per idea.
Validate using public information, local knowledge, rough economics, and practical
constraints.
Keep Facts, Assumptions, and Unknowns separate.
At minimum, check 3-5 competitors or alternatives, local price range, startup and monthly
costs, break-even volume, permits or legal constraints, and at least one external signal of
demand or the problem.
"Proceed" means worth developing into a Business Plan. It does not mean approved to
launch.



3. Business Plans
More than one idea can move forward to a Business Plan.
Create one copy of "TEMPLATE - Business Plan" per candidate business.
The Business Plan should explain why the business should exist, how it can succeed, and
how we will actually build and operate it.
Use the validation spreadsheet as the detailed numerical source; summarize only the key
numbers in the Business Plan.



4. Pitch Deck
Create a pitch deck only when there is a specific audience or need.
The deck should be derived from the Business Plan, not become a separate source of truth.




Folder Structure
   ●​ 01 Self Analysis - individual founder reflections and the AI prompt.
   ●​ 02 Business Ideas - one validation spreadsheet per idea.
   ●​ 03 Business Plans - one plan per candidate business.




Working Principles
   ●​   No need to force entrepreneurship as the answer.
   ●​   No need to select one idea immediately.
   ●​   Do not use arbitrary scores to choose ideas.
   ●​   Make important assumptions explicit.
   ●​   Prefer evidence over confidence.
   ●​   A business is a tool for achieving the life and impact we want, not the goal by itself.
